# Weekly portfolio briefing example

Fictional portfolio as of 17 November 2026.

## Attention required
Review blocked and at-risk projects first. For each, identify the specific dependency, the accountable owner and the next decision date. A project’s percent complete is a workload measure rather than an assurance that its next milestone is achievable.

## Reporting approach
Use the same reference date and source rows for all headline numbers. Keep source assessments visible. Export the filtered dataset when a stakeholder needs to investigate a figure. Record changes to a health assessment with an explanation rather than overwriting the history without context.

## Next review
Confirm whether blockers have an agreed resolution path, whether due dates remain achievable and whether a sponsor decision is outstanding. Reconcile the workbook and browser dashboard to the same CSV before distributing either.
